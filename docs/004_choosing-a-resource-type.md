# Choosing a Resource Type

Every resource you add has a **type** — `Article`, `Book`, `Support Group`, and so on. Choosing the type is the _first_ decision you make, and it matters because the type determines two things:

1. **Which fields you'll fill in.** A `Book` asks for an author and ISBN; a `Podcast` asks for Spotify and Apple links; a `Crisis Resource` asks for contact methods. The type tailors the editor to the thing.
2. **How the resource is displayed and grouped on the live site**. Resources are often shown together with others of the same type ("Books", "Support Groups"), so a mis-typed resource shows up in the wrong company.

Pick by asking: _what kind of thing is this, fundamentally?_ Below is a guide for every type, followed by help with the pairs people most often mix up.

| Type                  | Use it for                                                                                                                             |
| --------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Article**           | A single written piece — a standalone page such as that from a blog or other publication.                                              |
| **Blog**              | An ongoing blog, or a blog as a whole (not a single post).                                                                             |
| **Story**             | A personal, first-person account of grief and lived experience.                                                                        |
| **Listicle**          | A "list of…" piece ("10 books on child loss"). **Caution:** refers to lists of resources, not something like "10 Signs Of Depression". |
| **Book**              | A book, in any format.                                                                                                                 |
| **Printed Material**  | A downloadable/printable document — a PDF workbook, leaflet, guide.                                                                    |
| **Podcast**           | A podcast _show_ as a whole.                                                                                                           |
| **Podcast Episode**   | A single episode of a podcast.                                                                                                         |
| **Video**             | A single video (e.g. a talk, an explainer).                                                                                            |
| **Webinar**           | A recorded or scheduled webinar / online presentation.                                                                                 |
| **Course**            | A structured course or program.                                                                                                        |
| **App**               | A mobile or web app.                                                                                                                   |
| **Support Group**     | An organised, ongoing group people join for mutual support.                                                                            |
| **Peer Support**      | One-to-one or peer-led support (e.g. a buddy/mentor scheme).                                                                           |
| **Therapy Resource**  | Professional therapy or counselling services.                                                                                          |
| **Community**         | An online community or membership space.                                                                                               |
| **Forum**             | A discussion forum / message board.                                                                                                    |
| **Memorial**          | An online memorial or remembrance space.                                                                                               |
| **Crisis Resource**   | Urgent, safety-critical help — a hotline, text line, emergency service.                                                                |
| **Essential Service** | Practical, non-grief-specific help someone in crisis may need — food, shelter, financial aid.                                          |
| **External Org**      | A specific organization. Also used as the **Source** of other resources.                                                               |

## Sorting out the confusing pairs

Some types can be easily confused. Here's how to tell them apart.

**Podcast vs Podcast Episode.** Use **Podcast** for the show itself ("The Grief Cast"). Use **Podcast Episode** only when you're pointing at _one specific episode_ that's worth surfacing on its own. Link back to its parent Podcast if we have one, but it may be a grief related episode in an otherwise non-grief focused show.

**Article vs Blog vs Story vs Listicle.**

- **Article** — a single, self-contained piece of guidance or perhaps an opinion piece. Think a single blog post from an expert or an article from an online publication.
- **Blog** — an ongoing publication, when you're recommending the blog as a whole rather than a single post.
- **Story** — the defining feature is that it's _personal and first-person_: someone telling their own grief experience.
- **Listicle** — the piece's whole purpose is to be a _list of things_ ("7 apps for…"). If it reads more like an essay that happens to have some bullet points, it's an Article. The main thing to watch for are articles with titles like "10 Signs You Need Support" which are technically listicles - but we reserve our listicles for lists of resources

**Support Group vs Peer Support vs Community vs Forum vs Therapy Resource.**

- **Support Group** — an organised group you join and attend, with mutual support among members.
- **Peer Support** — support from another person with lived experience, often one-to-one (a mentor, a buddy line).
- **Community** — a broader online membership space; less structured than a group.
- **Forum** — specifically a message board / threaded discussion.
- **Therapy Resource** — involves a _professional_ (therapist, counsellor). If a qualified professional is providing the support, it's a Therapy Resource, not a Support Group.

**Crisis Resource vs Essential Service.**

- **Crisis Resource** — for _safety-critical, urgent_ help: suicide/crisis lines, emergency contacts. These are handled with extra care on the site.
- **Essential Service** — practical help that isn't grief-specific but a grieving person may urgently need: food banks, housing help, financial assistance.

**When something is an organisation.** If the resource _is_ an organisation that runs grief support, use **External Org**. External Orgs do double duty: they're also the values you pick in the **Source** field on other resources, to record which organisation an article or group belongs to. So if you find a great article on a charity's website, the article is an `Article` and the charity is an `External Org` set as its Source.

## Still not sure?

Two rules of thumb:

- **Pick the most specific type that fits.** "Article" is the safe fallback, but a more specific type gives visitors better grouping and gives you the right fields.
- **If nothing fits, don't force it.** A resource that resists every type may be a sign the content model needs a new type — raise it with a developer rather than forcing it into the closest approximation.

Once you've chosen a type, head to **Adding & Editing a Resource** for advice on filling it in.
