# Welcome to Why Grief Matters CMS

This is the content management system for [whygriefmatters.org](https://whygriefmatters.org).

> **Important**
>
> If you are new to Sanity, it might be worth [reading what the platform is](https://www.sanity.io/docs/getting-started/the-sanity-content-operating-system-an-introduction) to give yourself a good foundation on the concepts discussed throughout these guides

## Finding your way around the Studio

Sanity Studio is split into three main areas:

### The "Content" Pane

The left-hand most navigation lists all of the different kinds of content available within the project. It is subdivided into the following categories:

1. **Settings** — one-of-a-kind documents reserved for "special" cases, such as describing attributes relating to our organization
2. **Internet Resources** — documents describing the internet resources themselves. This is the bulk of our work
3. **Classification** — the documents we use to classify our internet resources
4. **Page-building Documents** — the various content types that we use to build out page templates on the website

### The "Document List" Pane

Usually the second pane along, the "document list" pane lists out all documents for the content type you have selected in the "content pane"

### The "Document" Pane

The main document editor - used to edit the fields of a specific document once selected from the list. What you see in this editor will vary depending on the type of content you have selected.

> **Note**
>
> In the most simple workflow, there will only ever be 3-panes. When editing complex document types, such as those that reference other documents, there may be multiple panes "stacked" left-to-right. Additional panes always open on the right making it easier to navigate deeply nested content

## How these guides are organised

Read them roughly in order if you're new; dip in as needed once you're comfortable.

- **The Content Model** — the vocabulary of the whole project.
- **The Role of a Content Editor** — what the job really is, and why judgement
  matters.
- **Choosing a Resource Type** — the first decision for every new resource.
- **Adding & Editing a Resource** — the day-to-day workflow, field by field.
- **Tagging & Classification** — the editorial heart of the project.
- **AI Assist Features** — working with the automated AI Content Editor.
- **Writing Descriptions & Titles** — voice and tone for a grieving audience.
- **Images & Accessibility** — alt text and image licensing.
- **Page Building** — composing hand-built pages (a more advanced task).
- **How Content Reaches the Site** — publishing, builds, and why changes aren't
  instant.
- **Glossary & FAQ** — quick reference for terms and common questions.

## Who to ask

- Questions about **what a tag means or where a boundary lies** go to the project's
  **editorial lead** — taxonomy scope is an editorial decision.
- Questions about a **missing field or type, a slug change, or a build/technical
  problem** go to a **developer**.
